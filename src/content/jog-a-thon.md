## 13th Annual Jog-A-Thon!

We are asking all students to collect pledges and donations from family, friends, neighbors and businesses. Funds raised will go towards supporting our STEAM programs (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics).

Students can collect pledges via a secure website:

Go to [http://pledgestar.com/mbma](http://pledgestar.com/mbma)

Click “Not Registered For This Year’s Event”

Enter your name and email address, then click “Submit”

Follow the instructions on-screen to register students

When you finish, the system emails pledge requests to your family and friends, and allows them to make secure credit card donations online.

You get notified each time a pledge is made, and you can track your pledge progress online. (Call 1-888-598-7510 if you need assistance.)

The classroom with the highest participation will win a JAMBA JUICE party!

Every student will receive a fun t-shirt to wear for the run!

In exchange for a tax-deductible donation of $250, families and businesses will get their name printed on the back of our annual Jog-A-Thon shirt. Please consider donating to help support our students and our school programs. Now is a great time to start making a list of family, friends and businesses you patron that can help you Leave Your Lasting Footprint!

As always, the Parents Group thanks you for your continued support.
