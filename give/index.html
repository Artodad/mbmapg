Box Tops

This program goes directly to the school

You can find Box Tops on many products, such as cereal, Kleenex, copy paper, granola bars, and much more. For years families have been clipping box tops and turning them into schools to earn money for the school. Each box top is worth 10 cents. Participation is FREE. The more participants, the more the school will benefit. Box Tops with an expiration date must be cut out and turned into the school office. We process these and submit them to earn funds. This traditional style of box top is being phased out and you will begin to see a new digital version. For a time, you will find both types on your favorite products. Please be sure to continue to cut out those with an expiration date and turn into the school office.

With this new digital version, Box Tops will be earned through the new mobile app.

Load the Mobile App

Create your account

Select MISSION BAY MONTESSORI as your school of choice

Follow the directions to scan your receipt - be sure to scan it within 14 days.

Earnings will automatically be added the school’s account.
