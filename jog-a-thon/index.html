More Information

What is Jog-A-Thon?
This is an event where the students come together, collect pledges and run to raise money to benefit their school.
What will the proceeds support?
Our STEAM Programs!
(Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics)
When is the Jog-A-Thon?
Friday, April 5, 2024
Starting at 8:30am
What do the runners do?
The students collect pledges from friends, family and businesses who will support their run. Due to Covid-19, this year all the pledges will be collected via the pledge star website.

Students can collect pledges via a secure website
• Go to http://pledgestar.com/mbma
• Click “Not Registered For This Year’s Event”
• Enter your name and email address, then click “Submit”
• Follow the instructions on-screen to register students
What is a pledge?
A pledge is a fixed fee donation collected by students for their participation in the Jog-A-Thon
How far will the kids run?
The kids will run as many laps as they can in a given amount of time. To ensure safety, the younger kids will run separately from the older kids and will run a shorter distance.
What do the kids need to bring for the event?
Each participant will receive a Jog-A-Thon T-shirt. Students should bring a water bottle, sunscreen and wear running shoes.
