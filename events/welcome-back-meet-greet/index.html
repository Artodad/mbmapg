Welcome Back Meet & Greet

Sat, Aug 22 | San Diego

Join us at Standley Park as we welcome in the new school year.

Time & Location

Aug 22, 2026, 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

San Diego, 3585 Governor Dr, San Diego, CA 92122, USA

About the event

Join us at Standley Park for our annual Welcome Back Meet & Greet, open to all new and returning MBMA families! This is a great opportunity to connect with our school community, learn about the exciting events and activities we have planned for the year, and sign up for volunteer opportunities and our popular Pizza Lunch program. Enjoy light snacks, meet fellow parents, and let the kids play at the playground while we kick off another fantastic year together.
